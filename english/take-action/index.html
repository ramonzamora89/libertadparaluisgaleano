Alert:

  • Luis Galeano has been held by ICE since September 14, 2026.
  • He has an immigration court hearing scheduled for October 2.
  • CPJ and 14 partner organizations call for his release. Read the statement
  • IAPA urges the U.S. not to deport him. Read more
