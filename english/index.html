Exiled Nicaraguan journalist

Free Luis Galeano Journalism is not a crime

Luis Galeano fled Nicaragua in 2018, persecuted by the regime of Daniel Ortega and Rosario Murillo, and sought asylum in the United States. Seven years later, his claim is still pending. On September 14, 2026, ICE detained him in Orlando, Florida. He now faces deportation proceedings.

The case

He fled a dictatorship. Now he could be sent back to it.

Luis Galeano, 48, runs Café con Voz, an opinion program he founded in Nicaragua in 2012 and still produces from exile. He left the country in December 2018, on the same day police raided 100% Noticias, the channel that aired his program. A Managua judge ordered his arrest.

In 2023, the Ortega regime stripped him of his citizenship and confiscated his property. In the United States, his asylum claim has been pending since June 17, 2019. Even so, ICE agents detained him while he was working as a rideshare driver.
